## What's DEFERRED and WHY (the key state to understand)

The only deterministic gate (smoke_projection_lens CPU+CUDA) exercises
**`realize_for_random_init` ONLY** — B=1, non-llama3, no-LoRA,
untied=true, weight_dtype=0, ZERO `tnn_gguf_*` calls. So all of the
following are NOT behaviorally gated and were deliberately NOT touched:

- **`realize_for_q8_copy`** — entire GGUF body. ZERO fixtures exist.
  Hard-deferred until a Q8 writer + round-trip gate exists.
- **`realize_for_mmap`** — GGUF mmap alloc/load (offset+stride math,
  LoRA naming). Deferred until a fused-name F32 round-trip gate exists.
- **`realize_for_full_finetune`** — its GGUF weight-LOAD half deferred.
  (Its ALLOC half is structurally gated, but unifying it with Step 4's
  block method is blocked by the w_o `[d_model,d_model]` vs
  `[n_heads*d_head]` shape divergence — GQA-latent, the gate can't catch
  it.)
- **llama3 rope_freq_factors / qkv_bias branches** — present in all
  paths but FALSE under the gate (smoke is non-llama3, qkv_bias=false),
  so un-gated even within random_init. Relocated verbatim, internals
  untouched. Deferred until a llama3 / qkv_bias fixture exists.
- **B>1 attn-mask body** — smoke is B=1; the B>1 body is unexercised.
  Deferred until a B>1 smoke exists.
- **Duplicated inline Mat-based Adam-zero** in q8 + mmap — tempting to
  unify onto `ft_zero_init_adam`, but both are un-gated. Deferred.
