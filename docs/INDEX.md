## Capability vs fixture — the seam

toy is a framework **built for ML research**, so it carries more than a
train/infer/serve surface. Two kinds of thing live here, and telling them apart
is the fastest way to read the repo:

**Capabilities** are framework features. Alternative credit assignment
(`--policy chain|dfa|frozen`), the feedback-matrix family (`DfaB`,
Kolen–Pollack, nDFA, LDFA), optimizers, LR schedules and controllers,
checkpointing, the error-conditioning instrument. These are documented as
features, gated like any other, and available to anyone.

**Fixtures** are the eleven synthetic task lanes — `mlp ctr gnn ssm lstm gtx
diff difflm ae franken franken-moe`. They exist so toy can test its capabilities
*independently of any one research question*. They are supported and gated, but
they are not the product path and a newcomer never needs them.
